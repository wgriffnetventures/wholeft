Common Questions

How do I recover my admin PIN?

Export a backup from the admin panel — your PIN is stored in the backup file. If you never exported a backup, you can reset the app by deleting and reinstalling it (this will clear all data).

How do I move my data to a new device?

Use Export Backup in the admin panel to save a backup file, then use Import Backup on your new device.

How do I cancel my WhoLeft Plus subscription?

On iPhone/iPad: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → WhoLeft.
On Android: Google Play → Subscriptions → WhoLeft.

The app isn't working as expected.

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, email us and we'll get back to you.
