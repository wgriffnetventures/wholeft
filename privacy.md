# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

WhoLeft is a classroom hall pass tracker available on Android, iOS, and as a web app at app.usewholeft.com.

## Data we collect

WhoLeft does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data entered into the app — including student names, PINs, and activity logs — is stored locally on your device only.

## Student data

WhoLeft is designed for classroom use. Student names entered into the app are stored only on the device running the app and are never transmitted to any third party.

## Purchases

WhoLeft Plus is a paid subscription processed separately per platform. We do not collect or store payment information. Purchases are handled by the respective platform's payment processor.

## Third party services

WhoLeft uses third party services to manage in-app purchases and payment processing. These services may collect anonymous purchase analytics in accordance with their own privacy policies.
