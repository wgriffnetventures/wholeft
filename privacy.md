  Privacy Policy

  Last updated: June 6, 2026

  WhoLeft is a classroom hall pass tracker for Android.

  Data we collect

  WhoLeft does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data entered into the app — including student names, PINs, and activity logs — is
  stored locally on your device only.

  Student data

  WhoLeft is designed for classroom use. Student names entered into the app are stored only on the device running the app and are never transmitted to any third party.

  Purchases

  WhoLeft Plus is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store payment information. Purchase records are managed entirely by Google.

  Third party services

  WhoLeft uses RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases. RevenueCat may collect anonymous purchase analytics. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.
